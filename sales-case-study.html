Sales Performance Analysis

Data Analytics Portfolio Project

Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance using a dataset of 1,500 sales orders. The analysis focuses on sales, profit, product performance, regional performance, and monthly trends.

The goal was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using data cleaning, Excel analysis, pivot tables, and dashboard visualization.

Business Problem

The business needed a clear way to understand sales performance across products, regions, and months. The analysis was designed to answer questions such as:

  • Which products generate the most sales?
  • Which regions perform the strongest?
  • Which months have the highest and lowest sales?
  • What is the overall profit performance?
  • What is the average order value?

Tools & Skills

Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables Data Cleaning Data Analysis Dashboard Design Business Insights

Dataset & Data Cleaning

The dataset contains 1,500 sales transactions with the following fields:

  • Order ID
  • Order Date
  • Customer ID
  • Product
  • Category
  • Region
  • Quantity
  • Discount
  • Sales
  • Profit

Data preparation included checking for blank values, duplicate Order IDs, formatting inconsistencies, dates, and numerical values before performing the analysis.

Dashboard

The dashboard provides a visual overview of sales, profit, orders, regional performance, monthly trends, and product performance.

Key Insights

  • The business generated total sales of ₹1,023,055.49 with a profit of ₹188,804.55.
  • West was the highest-performing region by sales, while Central recorded the lowest regional sales.
  • December recorded the highest monthly sales, while March recorded the lowest.
  • Laptop Pro 14 was the top-selling product, followed by Laptop Air 13 and Standing Desk.
  • The overall profit margin was 18.45%.
  • The average order value was ₹682.04.

Analysis Process

  1. Collected and reviewed the sales dataset.
  2. Checked data quality and formatting.
  3. Verified blanks and duplicate Order IDs.
  4. Created pivot tables for analysis.
  5. Calculated key sales and profit metrics.
  6. Analyzed regional and monthly performance.
  7. Identified top-performing products.
  8. Created the final sales dashboard.
